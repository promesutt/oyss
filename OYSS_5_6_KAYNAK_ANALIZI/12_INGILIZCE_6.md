## 2. Ders Kitabı İçerik ve Ünite Mimarisi

6. Sınıf İngilizce Ders Kitabı'nda 1. Dönem 1. Yazılı konuları 1. ve 2. Temalarda yer almaktadır:

### Theme 1: School Life (Sayfa 9 - 24)
- **Functions & Grammar:** Describing ongoing actions (Present Continuous / Progressive Tense), comparing daily routines and ongoing actions (Simple Present vs Present Progressive), expressing frequency (how often, adverbs of frequency: always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never).
- **Vocabulary:** School staff, laboratory assistant, headteacher, school facilities, celebration days (Republic Day, 23rd April Children's Day), daily routines.

### Theme 2: Classroom Life (Sayfa 25 - 40)
- **Functions & Grammar:** Confirming general truths and ongoing actions (Question tags: "You like Maths, don't you?", "She is writing, isn't she?"), showing possession (possessive adjectives: my, your, his, her, its, our, their; possessive pronouns), word order with adverbs.
- **Vocabulary:** Classroom rules, stationery, school subjects, timetable, projects.

### Theme 3: Personal Life (Sayfa 41 - 56)
- Physical appearance, body parts, personality adjectives, clothing styles.

### Theme 4: Family Life (Sayfa 57 - 72)
- Occupations and working routines, different types of houses, family relationships.
